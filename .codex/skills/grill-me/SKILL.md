# Grill Me

Use this repo-local skill to pressure-test a plan, design, architecture, or
understanding of existing product behavior before implementation.

## Core Behavior

- Ask one question at a time.
- Explore the repo first for discoverable answers.
- Include a recommended answer with every question.
- Keep the questioning scoped to the current pre-implementation phase.
- Use this when raw requirement intake or analysis still has unclear concepts,
  missing business rules, or terms that cannot be confirmed from the docs or
  codebase.
